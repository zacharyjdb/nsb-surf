# Design inspiration

Reference images for the app's look. The images are other people's work, so they stay local
(git-ignored); only this note is committed.

- `blue-water-tracker.png`: a water-tracker app concept. What we took from it: cool white-blue
  background, plain white cards with big soft corners, one bright blue accent, navy text, pale-blue
  chips and pills, blue bar charts, a wave-filled shape as the hero visual.
- `tide-card.png`: a tide card. Big current height with a rising/falling arrow, one soft blue curve,
  highs and lows labeled with bold time over height, night hours shaded, a dot for now.
- `16-day-wind-strip.png`: a 16-day strip with three wind arrows under each day (morning, midday,
  afternoon). We color those arrows by wind speed, Windy-style: light blue when calm, through green
  and yellow, to orange, red and magenta when it's howling.

## App icon direction (2026-11-14)

- `icon-carved-wave.png`, `icon-sun-on-water.png`, `icon-wave-bands-poster.png`, `icon-wavy-stripes.png`
- Flat, two colors only: warm cream (~#EFE8D8) and ocean blue (~#2F5FA8 / #3B6CC4). No gradients or gloss.
- Hand-drawn feel: slightly wobbly wave bands, a curl at the end of a band, a half sun sitting on the water.
- Faint print/risograph grain.

## DUE EAST logo direction (2026-11-15)

Replaces the cream print style above for the icon.

- `logo-dashed-wave-flag.png`, `logo-three-waves-on-water.png`, `logo-mountain-sea-blocks.png`, `logo-new-wave.png`
- Clean, geometric brand marks: stacked rounded wave strokes, some broken into dashes.
- Royal blue (~#2457E6), sky blue (~#4FB6E6), navy (~#15305A), white or pale-blue ground.

## Shark-tooth direction (2026-11-16)

Supersedes the wave-arrow icon. Reference only: this is a real brand's artwork (Griffins Brand "Due East"), so take the mood and don't copy it.

- `due-east-jaws-hat.png`, `due-east-inlet-tooth.png`
- Black ink on off-white, screen-print grain, a shark tooth or jaws motif, and chunky 70s-style bubble lettering.

## Compass-bearing direction (2026-11-16)

The icon has no letters; the app is still named DUE EAST. The heading points east (090°).

- `compass-rounded-arcs.png`, `compass-dial-light.png`, `compass-dial-navy.png`
- White on soft blue (powder ~#8FB2DA, slate ~#93A3B5, navy ~#33456B): tick-mark dials, a heading marker and rounded concentric arcs.

## Bold flat marks (2026-11-16, later)

Replaces the compass-bezel icon direction.

- `mark-wave-panel*.png`, `mark-banded-block.png`, `mark-brush-waves.png`, `mark-sunrise-stripes.png`
- Single-color marks: cream on deep earthy grounds (oxblood, olive) or black on cream.
- Organic, slightly rough linework: flowing wave contours, wavy cuts through a block, brush strokes, and a sunrise over a striped sea.
